A Letter From Your Brother ❤️

My Dearest Sister, ❤️

Today is Raksha Bandhan, but for me, this day is more than just tying a Rakhi.

It reminds me how lucky I am to have a sister like you in my life. You are the person who can make ordinary moments feel special just by being there. From our silly fights and endless teasing to the moments when we quietly support each other — every little memory with you means a lot to me. 🥹❤️

We may fight over the smallest things, annoy each other, and sometimes pretend that we don't care… but deep down, you know I'll always be there for you.

Whenever you need someone to listen, someone to stand beside you, or simply someone to annoy you again 😄 — your brother will always be there.

No matter how much we grow, where life takes us, or how busy we become, I never want this beautiful bond between us to change.

This Rakhi, I don't promise that I'll never tease you… because that's impossible. 😂

But I do promise one thing:
Whenever you need me, I'll always have your back. ❤️

You are not just my sister. You are a beautiful part of my life, one of my biggest blessings, and someone I will always protect and love.

So today, as you tie this Rakhi on my wrist, remember that this little thread carries something much bigger — a lifetime of memories, love, laughter, and an unbreakable bond between a brother and his sister. 🎀❤️

Happy Raksha Bandhan, My Beautiful Sister! 🌸

May you always keep smiling, keep shining, and achieve everything you dream of.

With endless love,

Your Brother ❤️
